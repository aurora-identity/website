<section class="section-card" id="overview">
  <h2>Overview</h2>
  <p>
    Aurora is an identity-risk intelligence layer that shows which <strong>user identities</strong> are accessing AI tools — as well as developer, HR, data, and financial systems — by analysing browser telemetry, network logs, or emails inside your environment.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="section-card" id="terms">
  <h3>User identities</h3>
  <p>
    Refer to identities associated with humans within an identity system. Also known as human, workforce, or individual identities. These are the identities Aurora Identity focuses on today.
  </p>

  <h3>AI (or non-human) identities</h3>
  <p>
    Refer to non-human identities, such as service accounts, machines, workloads, or AI/autonomous agents. These are the identities Aurora Identity is currently exploring and will focus on next.
  </p>
</section>
