<section class="section-card" id="overview">
  <h2>Reporting dashboard</h2>
  <p>
    The reporting dashboard gives security teams a consolidated view of AI and SaaS activity mapped to identities. It highlights risky behaviour and unmanaged identities, so you can prioritize investigation and remediation.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="section-card" id="insights">
  <h2>What you can observe</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Identity-level context:</strong> Correlates browser or network signals to show who is using which tools.</li>
    <li><strong>Risk scoring:</strong> Surfaces risky services, unusual behaviour, and unmanaged identities for faster triage.</li>
    <li><strong>Service coverage:</strong> Tracks AI tooling alongside critical developer, HR, data, and finance applications.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section-card" id="workflow">
  <h2>How teams use it</h2>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Monitor</strong> the overview for spikes in risky activity across AI and SaaS services.</li>
    <li><strong>Drill down</strong> into identities or services with elevated scores to understand exposure paths.</li>
    <li><strong>Act</strong> by opening an investigation based on context kept inside your tenant.</li>
  </ol>
</section>
